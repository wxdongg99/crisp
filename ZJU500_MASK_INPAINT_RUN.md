## Observation

- The 500-mask directory contains 256×256 masks generated by the local
  pipeline; several are large strips or blobs, not fine defects.
- Inpainting preserves background and only edits the mask support.
- With CFG=12 the mask region often becomes a fabric fold/wrinkle or edge
  artifact; it does not consistently look like a real defect.
- This is still consistent with the token/mask quality gate failing: the
  protocol is correct, the content is not yet convincing.
